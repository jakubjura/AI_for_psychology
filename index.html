Research focus

AI as a thoughtful second perspective

We develop tools that help professionals see complex material more clearly—from educational-psychological assessment to psychotherapy supervision. Our systems bring structured analysis and multiple perspectives into the workflow while keeping expert judgment at the center.

Projects

Two complementary research directions connect interpretable AI workflows with the day-to-day needs of psychologists, educators, and psychotherapists.

01
Diagnostics Special education Hybrid AI

SYNDI — LLM Synthesis for Special Education Diagnostic Reports

AI-assisted diagnostic framework for special education designed to provide an objective “second opinion” based on standardized methodologies. By integrating clinical data and PorTeX test results, the hybrid system (ANN/rules + LLM) synthesizes diagnostic conclusions and practical recommendations for schools and parents. Its primary goal is to reduce administrative workload and increase the objectivity and standardization of the diagnostic process.

02
Psychotherapy Multi-agent debate LLM

MAD AI Supervision

LLM-based multi-agent debate system for the supervision of psychotherapy sessions. It combines transcript correction, speaker diarization, and prosodic, thematic, and linguistic analysis with multiple independent supervisory perspectives, including systemic, psychodynamic, cognitive-behavioral, humanistic/gestalt, and Ericksonian approaches. A final Multi-Agent Debate integrates these perspectives to provide a broader, multi-theoretical view of the therapeutic process.

Human-in-the-loop

Designed around professional judgment

The diagnostic workflow connects assessment data, AI-supported synthesis, report preparation, and practical outputs for families and schools.

The central safeguard is simple: the professional reviews and approves every AI-generated output. The technology supports routine synthesis; it does not replace clinical responsibility.
